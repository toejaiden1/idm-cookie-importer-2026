## FAQ

**Does it work for Udemy and Coursera downloads?**  
Yes — import your logged-in browser cookies and IDM can download course videos.

**How often do I need to refresh cookies?**  
Cookies expire at different rates. Most session cookies last 7–30 days before you need to re-import.
